Task foundry

A company's data export goes in. Agents prune it, gather documented ways coding agents fail, and write task families over the data whose rules exercise those failures. Each family starts at a level the model cannot pass and comes down until the untrained model passes about half the headline tasks and a quarter of the harder ones. Qwen is then trained on its own graded-correct answers and benchmarked again on held-out tasks.
