Task foundry

Agents read a business data export, gather developer complaints and papers on how coding agents fail, and stack those failures into tasks over the data. Each task starts at a level no model can pass and comes down one rule at a time until the pass rate reaches about 25%. Then Qwen is trained on the tasks and benchmarked again.
